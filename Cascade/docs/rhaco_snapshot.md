# RHACO data snapshot (`src/data/rhaco_snapshot.json`)

> **Operator review required before this repository goes public.** The snapshot
> was generated by an automated agent from a live observatory tree. The observatory
> operator must check the JSON, this description and the generator script before
> any public push, and confirm that the aggregates may be published.

Cascade shows a small amount of real data next to its simulated air showers. That
data comes from the **RHACO citizen observatory**, a single-detector cosmic-ray and
radiation station in the Great Basin of northern Nevada. The snapshot contains
**daily aggregates only**. It has no per-event identifiers or event timestamps, no
device serial numbers, no network addresses, no file paths, no host names and no
coordinates.

It is regenerated with:

```
python scripts/rhaco_snapshot.py <RHACO_ROOT> --end-day YYYY-MM-DD --out src/data/rhaco_snapshot.json
```

The script reads the observatory tree without writing to it. It takes the root as a
required argument, so the script itself contains no machine paths. It needs pandas
only for the optional gamma pass (use `--no-gamma` to skip that pass).

## Honest scope (applies to every field)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Detector | RadiaCode RC-110, CsI(Tl) scintillator, 1 cm^3. One unit, the castle-shielded primary |
| Shielding | Hand-built graded-Z lead castle, 30 mm walls / 40 mm lid |
| Configuration era | Sealed castle since 2026-05-20 23:11:04 UTC: lid notched, USB cable through a labyrinth seal. It is close to, but not, full 4 pi shielding |
| Window | 2026-05-20 23:11:04 UTC through the end of 2026-11-26 UTC (the last complete UTC day at generation). All dates are UTC calendar days |
| Site | 1539 m (5050 ft) above sea level, atmospheric depth ~840 g/cm^2. The documented site pressure reference is ~844.1 hPa |
| Baseline anchors (documented by the observatory, quoted, not re-derived) | Gamma channel 0.7070 cps (locked 2026-06). Cosmic-ray overflow channel 0.07021 +- 0.0002 cps (locked 2026-06, first post-reset week excluded). Barometric coefficient -0.25 %/hPa |
| Classifier | Unrecorded for 2026-05-21 to 05-24 (the provenance columns were added on 2026-05-24). Then v3.6.1 until 2026-07-05, and v3.7.0 from 2026-07-05/06 onward. The versions are recorded per day in `event_class_daily.classifier_version` |
| Event-log schema | Unrecorded, then v1.0, then v1.1 and v1.2 (both on 2026-07-05), also per day. This is the logger's event-log schema version, not this JSON's `schema` field |

**Measurement defines category.** Every number below is something the instrument or
its classifier *measured* or *labelled*. The firmware label, the classifier category
and the physical interpretation are three different layers. The app must not merge
them into one causal claim (for example, "an EAS event is an air shower that hit the
detector").

## Fields

### `provenance`

This section holds the generation time (UTC), a plain-language description of the
sources, the detector, shielding and configuration era, the window, the
overflow-accumulator reset instant, the end of the settling week, and the
classifier and schema versions seen in the window. It also repeats the documented
baselines and the site description, and sets `operator_review_required: true`.
Credit line: "RHACO citizen observatory".

### `overflow_daily`: the cosmic-ray overflow channel

- **What is measured.** The detector keeps a cumulative 1024-channel energy spectrum
  on board, and the logger saves a copy about every 5 minutes. The last channel,
  ch1023, is an overflow bin: it holds every deposit above ~2.78 MeV.
- **How the daily rate is computed.** Consecutive saved spectra are differenced
  (change in ch1023 counts divided by change in the device-reported accumulation
  time). Each difference is assigned to the UTC day that contains its midpoint, and
  the differences are summed per day.
  - `rate_cps = counts / accum_seconds`.
  - `rate_err_cps = sqrt(counts) / accum_seconds` (Poisson only).
  - `coverage = accum_seconds / 86400`.
- **Gaps and resets.** One accumulator reset (2026-05-22 12:25 UTC) is skipped.
  Four pairs of spectra more than 1 h apart (about 2.4 days of accumulation) are
  also skipped, so those days have low `coverage`.
- **Settling week.** `settling: true` marks days up to 2026-05-29. The observatory
  treats the first post-reset week as a settling transient (week-1 rate ~0.0724 cps)
  and excludes it from its reference.
- **Era mean.** `overflow_summary` gives the mean over the 180 post-settling days:
  0.069777 +- 0.000067 cps. That is about 0.6% (6 sigma) below the locked 0.07021
  reference, which was set over 2026-05 to 06.
- **The seconds are not dead-time corrected.** `accum_seconds` is the device's
  spectrum duration. It is not confirmed to be live time with dead-time correction,
  so the app should treat it as accumulation time.
- **Caveat: this is not a muon count.** The overflow bin takes *any* deposit above
  2.78 MeV in the crystal. That is dominated by cosmic-ray secondaries (mostly
  muons) passing through the 1 cm^3 crystal, but it is not a muon identification.
  It also contains other high-energy deposits and any pile-up.

### `pressure_daily` and `barometric_regression`

- **What is measured.** Station (absolute) pressure from the site's weather station,
  logged about every 60 s. The fields are the daily mean, minimum, maximum and
  sample count in hPa. The mean over the window is ~849 hPa, slightly above the
  documented ~844.1 hPa site reference.
- **Regression method.** A weighted least-squares fit of the daily overflow rate
  against daily mean pressure, weighted by 1/sigma_Poisson^2. It uses post-settling
  days only, with overflow coverage >= 0.90 and >= 1200 pressure samples (174 days,
  2026-05-30 to 2026-11-26, pressure 835 to 859 hPa). The coefficient is
  100 x slope / (rate at mean pressure), in percent per hPa.
- **Results.** Two fits are reported, each with a formal error and an error scaled
  by sqrt(chi2/dof):
  - pressure only: **-0.363 +- 0.032 %/hPa** (chi2/dof 1.27);
  - pressure plus a linear time trend: **-0.371 +- 0.030 %/hPa**.
- **Comparison with the documented value.** The observatory's documented coefficient
  is -0.25 %/hPa. The value measured here is steeper by about 3.5 sigma.
- **Caveats:**
  - Daily means only. There is no correction for atmospheric temperature
    (seasonal) or for heliospheric modulation (Forbush decreases). Both can
    correlate with pressure over a May to November window.
  - The fit uses the >2.78 MeV overflow channel. The documented -0.25 %/hPa may
    have been set for a different observable or method.
  - This is a single-detector measurement and has not been reviewed by the
    observatory. Present it as "measured in this snapshot", not as the station's
    coefficient.

### `event_class_daily`, `event_class_totals`, `event_log_diagnostics`

- **What is counted.** For each UTC day, the number of event-log rows of type
  `CLASSIFIED` for each class: MUON, MUON_SUB, RADON, EAS, SUPER-EAS and
  INCLINED-EAS.
- **What is left out:**
  - POISSON is excluded. It is below the classifier's 1.5x gate, so it is not an
    event.
  - `FW_ENRICHMENT` rows are not counted. Every one of them annotates an event that
    already has a `CLASSIFIED` row, so counting them would count those events twice.
  - Science-channel `SPIKE`/`DIP` trigger rows, `GAP` rows and other operational
    rows are not counted.
- **Coverage per day.** `science_coverage` is the logger-uptime proxy: 1 Hz science
  samples / 86400. The first day covers only 23:11 to 24:00 UTC. A logger outage
  left 2026-08-23 at ~5% coverage and 2026-08-24 at 0%, and 2026-05-28 is at ~73%.
- **Rates.** `event_class_totals` gives rates per calendar day and per live day
  (total / sum of coverage), with Poisson errors. The window has 186.75 live days.
  Per live day, with Poisson errors:

  | Class | Events per live day |
  |---|---|
  | MUON | 4.88 +- 0.16 |
  | MUON_SUB | 29.9 +- 0.4 |
  | RADON | 22.7 +- 0.35 |
  | EAS | 1.43 +- 0.09 |
  | SUPER-EAS | 0.81 +- 0.07 |
  | INCLINED-EAS clusters | 0.59 +- 0.06 |
- **What the classes are.** They are **classifier categories, defined by firmware-
  filtered count-rate ratios. They are not physical particle identifications.** The
  classifier is a decision tree evaluated in this order: POISSON -> RADON -> MUON ->
  SUPER-EAS -> EAS. The ratio is the trigger CPS divided by the 300-sample rolling
  median.
  - MUON: 1.5 to 8x, with a negative or flat post-event tail.
  - RADON: an elevated post-event tail below 15x. RADON is tested *before*
    MUON/EAS, so the EAS population below 15x is what remains after radon
    extraction, not a clean amplitude band.
  - EAS: 8 to 50x. SUPER-EAS: 50x or more.
  - MUON_SUB: an envelope the firmware event channel detected but the ratio
    classifier did not.
  - INCLINED-EAS: a cluster-level row, emitted when 3 or more firmware onsets fall
    within 30 s and no member reaches 15x. It is not a per-event class, and its
    members also carry their own classes.
- **Interpretation is separate from the label.** The observatory *interprets* EAS
  and SUPER-EAS as passages of a dense air-shower core, and INCLINED-EAS as an
  inclined shower. Those are interpretations layered on the label, not
  measurements. Some MUON_SUB rows share a timestamp with a MUON, RADON or EAS row
  from the science channel. These are co-emissions from two detection channels, and
  both are counted as the logger emitted them.

### `eas_ratio_histogram`

- **What it shows.** The classification ratio (trigger CPS / rolling-median CPS) for
  all EAS and SUPER-EAS rows in the window (419 events, median 23.2x, maximum
  ~395x). It uses 12 log-spaced bins from 8x to 300x, counted separately for each
  class, with under- and overflow counts.
- **EAS rows below 8x.** Five EAS rows have a ratio below 8x. The classifier's
  reclassify pass can re-evaluate against the current median, which explains how
  this can happen.
- **Saturation.** The CPS of the largest events is firmware-saturated, so the ratio
  is not a linear energy measure.
- **Classifier versions.** `classifier_versions` gives the version mix of the
  contributing rows.
- **The 50x boundary.** It sits in an empirical gap in the ratio distribution, and
  the observatory audited and retained it.

### `gamma_daily` (optional)

- **What is measured.** The daily mean and median of the 1 Hz firmware-filtered
  count rate (total CPS, all energies) from real-time samples, with sample counts.
- **Daily values include events and radon.** This is the total count rate, not a
  quiescent baseline. Radon episodes and events raise the daily *mean*; the
  *median* is more robust to them.
- **Comparison with the baseline.** The locked gamma baseline 0.7070 cps is a
  quiescent reference. It is not interchangeable with the daily mean.

## Things not included or not computed

- No per-event list, timestamps, identifiers or catalog IDs. This is by design.
- No neutron-monitor comparison and no Forbush-decrease analysis.
- No temperature correction of the overflow rate.
- The second, bench-mounted unshielded detector at the site is excluded. Its data
  must not be mixed with the castle data.
