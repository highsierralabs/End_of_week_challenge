End of week challenge

A Claude Code subscription's allowance resets once a week. Whatever is left of it before then goes into one build that Claude picks, designs and ships on its own: seven questions at the start, then no further direction.

The series runs on one Claude Code skill, /end-of-week-challenge, which lives in the repository. Read the skill or run your own series.
